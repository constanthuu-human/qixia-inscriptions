Objects · Places · Public life

Buddhist objects and places

Buddhism became visible in Nanjing through things that people could enter, touch, carry, see, repair, donate, and remember.

Where Buddhism became visible

A teaching needs a material setting.

An object is never only an object. Its meaning comes from the ritual, people, and place around it. These examples move from Qixia’s cliff sanctuary to a living printing tradition.

01 · Late fifth century · Southern Qi, with later additions · Rock-cut Buddhist image · 石窟造像

A Buddha niche at Qixia’s Thousand Buddha Cliff 栖霞山千佛岩佛龛

Qixia Mountain · 栖霞山

Offering and merit · 供养与功德

Modern photograph of surviving niches; the cliff is a layered site, not one untouched Southern Qi scene · 现存石窟佛龛现代摄影；千佛岩是累积形成的遗址，并非一处未经变化的南齐原貌。

Historical story

The Thousand Buddha Cliff is a group of niches cut into the mountain over generations. The earliest major carvings belong to the Southern Dynasties, but later patrons continued to add images, repair surfaces, and reshape the cliff as a place of worship. A niche was therefore never only a finished sculpture: it was a durable offer placed in a landscape that later communities could keep using.

千佛岩不是一座同时完成、容纳一千尊同样佛像的石窟，而是历代不断开凿、增补和礼拜的佛教石窟群。其早期重要造像属于南朝，但后来的信众继续在附近造像、修整石面，使山崖逐渐成为持续生成的宗教空间。

Value

A patron’s name could disappear while the carved image remained available for bowing, contemplation, teaching, and the dedication of merit. Stone made a private act of generosity public and durable.

施主的名字可能消失，但石刻仍可供礼拜、观想、听法与回向功德。石头把个人的布施转化为公开而持久的宗教行动。

Connections

Ming Sengshao and Fadu · 明僧绍与法度传统创建故事 · Foundation tradition

Temple traditions connect the elite recluse Ming Sengshao with the monk Fadu, whose community gave religious form to the mountain retreat.

栖霞寺传统把隐士明僧绍与僧人法度联系起来：前者提供社会与空间资源，后者使山中退居转向有组织的佛教共同体。

Ming Zhongzhang · 明仲璋家族延续 · Family continuation

Ming Sengshao’s son is remembered as continuing the project after his father’s death, showing how patronage could move through a household.

明僧绍去世后，其子明仲璋被记为继续这一项目，说明宗教赞助也可以通过家族关系延续。

Interesting story

A cliff made by accumulation · 由累积形成的山崖有趣之处 · An interesting detail

The site became a sacred landscape not because one founder completed it, but because later acts of carving made the first act visible again and again.

千佛岩成为圣境，不是因为一位创建者一次性完成了全部工程，而是因为后来的开凿不断让最初的宗教行动获得新的可见形式。

Evidence

The cliff itself · 山崖遗存考古与遗址 · Material evidence

Surviving niches, images, inscriptions, and differing styles show a site with multiple phases rather than a single moment of construction.

现存佛龛、造像、题刻与风格差异表明这里经历了多个阶段，而不是一次完成的单一工程。

Jiang Zong’s inscription · 江总碑文文字证据 · Textual evidence

The Qixia foundation story is preserved in a later commemorative voice that connects patrons, monks, and institutional beginnings.

栖霞创建故事保存在后来的纪念性文字中，文字把施主、僧人和寺院制度的开端连接起来。

02 · Southern Dynasties · probably beginning in the Liu Song · Ceramic roof-tile end · 陶质瓦当

A lotus-pattern tile from the Xiying Village temple 西营村南朝佛寺莲花纹瓦当

Xiying Village, Yuhuatai · 雨花台区西营村

Lotus and relic offering · 莲花与舍利供养

Comparative lotus eaves tile image; the excavated Xiying tile is represented by the archaeological record rather than this exact specimen · 莲花纹瓦当对比图；西营村出土的具体瓦当以考古报告为依据，并非此图中的同一件器物。

Historical story

A 瓦当 protected the end of a roof tile, but its repeated lotus pattern also helped make the temple recognizable. At Xiying Village, archaeologists found foundations, roof materials, column bases, image fragments, and a reliquary from a large Southern Dynasties Buddhist complex whose original name is not securely known. The wooden buildings disappeared; fired clay kept a trace of the institution above ground.

瓦当首先是保护屋檐瓦端的建筑构件，但反复出现的莲花纹也使寺院拥有可辨认的宗教面貌。西营村遗址出土了建筑基础、屋面材料、柱础、佛像残片和舍利容器，显示这里曾有一处规模较大的南朝佛教建筑群，但寺院原名尚不能确定。木构建筑消失了，烧制陶瓦却留下了制度存在的痕迹。

Value

The tile shows that Buddhist meaning could be built into ordinary architecture. Visitors encountered the lotus before reaching the image hall; the symbol was not confined to an altar.

这枚瓦当说明佛教意义可以进入日常建筑。访客还未进入佛殿，就已经在屋顶与檐口看见莲花；宗教象征并不只存在于佛像前。

Connections

Nanjing Institute of Archaeology · 南京市考古研究院发掘者 · Excavating institution

The institute’s excavation since 2019 recovered the architectural and ritual traces that written history had not preserved.

自2019年起，南京市考古研究院的发掘找回了文字史料没有保存的建筑与宗教活动线索。

Front pagoda, rear hall · 前塔后殿寺院格局 · Institutional layout

The pagoda stood before the main hall along a central axis, making relic worship an organizing feature of the complex.

塔位于主殿之前、处在中轴线上，使舍利崇拜成为寺院空间组织的重要因素。

Interesting story

A lost name, a surviving plan · 名称消失，格局尚存有趣之处 · An interesting detail

Archaeology can recover the size and arrangement of a monastery even when its historical name has not survived.

即使寺院的历史名称已经失传，考古仍能从遗址中复原它的大致规模与空间布局。

Evidence

Excavated architecture · 出土建筑遗存考古证据 · Archaeological evidence

The reported complex includes foundations, roof materials, column bases, Buddhist image fragments, and a stone reliquary.

报告所述遗址包括建筑基础、屋面材料、柱础、佛像残片与石质舍利容器。

A cautious date · 谨慎的年代判断仍需辨析 · Historical caution

The earliest phase may reach back to the Liu Song period, but the exact temple name and final destruction history remain unresolved.

遗址最早阶段可能追溯至刘宋，但寺院确切名称与最终毁弃过程仍未完全确定。

03 · Northern Song · 1011 · Miniature reliquary tower · 舍利容器塔

The Seven-Treasure Ashoka Pagoda 七宝阿育王塔

Changgan Temple underground chamber · 长干寺地宫

Relic presence · 舍利

Modern photograph of the Northern Song reliquary · 北宋舍利塔现代摄影。

Historical story

In 1011, a relic deposit was sealed beneath Changgan Temple. The pagoda was one layer in a nested system: an underground chamber, stone container, iron chest, decorated miniature tower, and smaller precious containers. In 2008, archaeologists working at the Grand Bao’en Temple site opened the chamber and brought the hidden object back into public study.

1011年，长干寺把舍利安置在地下并封存。七宝阿育王塔是层层保护系统中的一层：地下宫、石函、铁函、装饰华丽的小塔以及塔内更小的珍贵容器。2008年，大报恩寺遗址考古工作开启了这座地宫，使原本隐藏的器物重新进入公众研究的视野。

Value

The pagoda was sacred architecture in miniature. Its beauty was directed toward the relics inside, not toward ordinary display. Once sealed, it continued to mark the sacred status of the whole site while remaining unseen.

这座小塔是微缩的神圣建筑。它的华美首先服务于内部舍利，而不是日常陈列。地宫封闭后，舍利塔虽不可见，仍持续赋予整个遗址以神圣性。

Connections

Changgan Temple monks · 长干寺僧团宗教组织者 · Religious organizers

Monks coordinated the deposit, its ritual meaning, and the closing of the underground chamber.

僧团负责组织舍利安置、赋予仪式意义，并完成地宫的封闭。

Donors and artisans · 施主与工匠题记中的参与者 · Participants in inscriptions

Inscriptions preserve blessings and names connected with the people who contributed to the object’s creation.

塔上的题记保存了祝愿，也留下参与制作与供养者的名字。

Interesting story

The object was meant to disappear · 它本来就要消失在视线之外有趣之处 · An interesting detail

Its first public life was not display but concealment. The museum object seen today was designed to be hidden beneath a pagoda.

它最初的公共宗教生命不是展示，而是隐藏。今天在博物馆中看到的器物，原本就是为埋藏在塔下而制作的。

Evidence

The nested deposit · 层层套置的地宫考古证据 · Archaeological evidence

The sequence of chamber, stone container, iron chest, and miniature pagoda reveals both ritual hierarchy and technical protection.

地宫、石函、铁函与小塔的层层关系同时呈现了仪式等级与保护技术。

Twenty inscriptions · 二十则题记社会史料 · Social evidence

The inscriptions connect the sacred object to people, blessings, and networks of contribution rather than to an isolated workshop alone.

题记把这件圣物与具体人物、愿望和供养网络联系起来，而不是把它留在一个无名工坊的世界里。

04 · Ming dynasty · fifteenth century · Glazed architectural component · 琉璃建筑构件

A glazed architectural component from the Grand Bao’en Pagoda 大报恩寺琉璃塔构件

Grand Bao’en Temple, Nanjing · 南京大报恩寺

Repaying kindness · 报恩

Surviving glazed arch component photographed in a museum collection · 博物馆收藏的琉璃拱门残件摄影。

Historical story

The Yongle Emperor ordered the Grand Bao’en Temple and its pagoda in 1412; the project was completed in 1431 under the Xuande Emperor. A glazed component passed through clay preparation, moulding, firing, colouring, transport, and careful assembly. The pagoda was destroyed in 1856, but fragments and unused reserve pieces survived to show the workshops and labour behind an imperial Buddhist landmark.

永乐皇帝于1412年下令营建大报恩寺及其琉璃塔，工程于1431年宣德年间完成。一件琉璃构件要经过制泥、模制、烧制、上色、运输与精确安装。琉璃塔于1856年毁于战乱，但残件以及可能用于维修的备用构件保留下来，让我们看到这座帝国佛教地标背后的工坊与劳动。

Value

报恩 means acknowledging and repaying the kindness of parents, teachers, the Three Jewels, and other beings. At Bao’en Temple, a moral principle became a monumental public statement—and also a display of imperial resources.

“报恩”指对父母、师长、三宝以及一切有恩者的感念与回报。在大报恩寺，这一伦理原则被转化为巨大的公共建筑，同时也展示了帝国的资源与组织能力。

Connections

Zhu Di and the Ming court · 朱棣与明廷帝国赞助 · Imperial patronage

The court framed the temple as a memorial act for Zhu Yuanzhang and Empress Ma, tying Buddhist devotion to dynastic gratitude.

明廷把寺院解释为纪念朱元璋与马皇后的报恩行动，使佛教虔敬与王朝感恩相互连接。

Kilns, workshops, and laborers · 窑场、工坊与劳工隐藏的生产网络 · Production network

The tower depended on many workers whose individual names rarely appear in the monument’s public memory.

这座塔依靠大量工人完成，而他们的个人姓名很少出现在这座纪念性建筑的公共记忆中。

Interesting story

The fragment can reveal a whole tower · 残件可以讲述整座塔有趣之处 · An interesting detail

A curve, joint, or glaze colour can help researchers infer the form of an arch, the decoration of a storey, and the technical system of a vanished building.

一处弧度、榫接或釉色，都可能帮助研究者推测拱门形制、楼层装饰与一座消失建筑的技术系统。

Evidence

Surviving glazed pieces · 现存琉璃构件物质证据 · Material evidence

Dragons, celestial figures, elephants, lions, lotus flowers, and plant scrolls survive as parts of the pagoda’s decorative language.

龙、飞天、象、狮、莲花与卷草纹等残件保存了琉璃塔的装饰语言。

A building that vanished · 消失的建筑重建边界 · Limits of reconstruction

The pieces support a reconstruction of materials and decoration, but not every detail of the original tower’s complete appearance.

残件可以支持对材料与装饰的复原，但不能自动证明原塔外观的每一个细节。

05 · Early Ming dynasty, with later changes of use · Brick-vaulted hall · 砖拱殿堂

Linggu Temple’s Beamless Hall 灵谷寺无梁殿

Linggu Scenic Area, Zhongshan · 钟山灵谷景区

Infinite life · 无量寿

Modern interior photograph of a brick-vaulted hall; the building’s twentieth-century function also matters · 砖拱殿堂现代室内摄影；这座建筑在20世纪的功能变化同样是其历史的一部分。

Historical story

The Beamless Hall used massive brick arches instead of an ordinary timber beam system. It belonged to the early Ming reconstruction of Linggu Temple after the monastery was moved in 1381 to make way for the Xiaoling imperial mausoleum. When later conflict destroyed many timber buildings, the vaulted hall survived. In the Republican period, the former Buddhist complex became a memorial cemetery and the hall took on a new commemorative role.

无梁殿以厚重砖拱代替通常的木梁架结构。1381年，为给明孝陵让出空间，原寺院迁往钟山新址；无梁殿属于明初灵谷寺重建的一部分。后来的战争毁坏了许多木构建筑，砖拱殿堂却保存下来。民国时期，原佛寺区域转为纪念性墓园，无梁殿也承担了新的纪念功能。

Value

The hall’s original association with the Buddha of Infinite Life linked architecture to hopes about mortality and liberation. Its later use shows that a sacred building can survive physically while its community, images, and rituals change.

殿堂最初供奉无量寿佛，把建筑与对死亡、长寿和解脱的宗教思考联系起来。后来的改用说明：建筑可以继续存在，但使用它的共同体、内部图像与仪式都可能改变。

Connections

Zhu Yuanzhang · 朱元璋迁寺与帝陵 · Relocation and imperial mausoleum

The Hongwu emperor’s mausoleum project displaced the earlier monastery and reshaped the religious geography of Zhongshan.

洪武皇帝的陵墓工程促成原寺迁移，也重新塑造了钟山的宗教地理。

The later memorial institution · 后来的纪念机构功能转换 · Change of use

The Republican-era cemetery gave the hall a national memorial function without erasing its Buddhist architectural form.

民国时期的纪念性墓园赋予无梁殿新的国家纪念功能，却没有抹去它的佛教建筑形态。

Interesting story

‘Beamless’ is both structure and memory · “无梁”既是结构也是记忆有趣之处 · An interesting detail

The popular name points to a technical fact—its vaults—but also preserves the way later visitors understood the hall through a memorable phrase.

“无梁”这个名称既指向砖拱结构这一技术事实，也保存了后来的参观者如何用一个易记的词理解这座建筑。

Evidence

Brick vaults · 砖拱结构建筑证据 · Architectural evidence

The surviving vault explains why the hall was more resistant to the fires that consumed many timber structures around it.

保存下来的砖拱结构解释了为什么它比周围许多木构建筑更能抵抗火灾。

A changed function · 功能改变连续与断裂 · Continuity and rupture

The hall is evidence not of one uninterrupted religious use, but of a structure repeatedly given new meanings.

这座殿堂证明的不是一种从未中断的宗教用途，而是一座建筑如何不断获得新的意义。

06 · Qing dynasty to the present · Carved wooden printing block · 佛经雕版木版

A Buddhist sutra woodblock from the Jinling Scriptural Press 金陵刻经处佛经木版

Jinling Scriptural Press, Nanjing · 南京金陵刻经处

Giving the Dharma · 法布施

Modern photograph of preserved scripture blocks; it represents a continuing craft tradition, not one single Qing edition · 保存中的佛经木版现代摄影；它呈现的是延续至今的工艺传统，并非某一部清代经本的唯一原版。

Historical story

In 1866, Yang Renshan and collaborators established the Jinling Scriptural Press to recover, correct, carve, print, and circulate Buddhist texts. A block carries characters in reverse; calligraphy, collation, carving, proofing, printing, and binding all stand between a manuscript and a reader. The press therefore joined Buddhist learning to craft labor and public distribution.

1866年，杨仁山与同道创办金陵刻经处，搜集、校勘、雕刻、印刷并传播佛教经典。木版上的汉字是反刻的，从手稿到读者之间还要经过书法摹写、文字校勘、雕刻、试印与装订。金陵刻经处因此把佛教学问与工匠劳动、公共传播连接起来。

Value

法布施 means sharing the Dharma. Unlike a single gift that is used once, a block can produce many copies that travel to temples, schools, libraries, and individual readers. The object is a tool for making knowledge mobile.

“法布施”指传播佛法。与一次性使用的物质布施不同，一块木版可以反复印出经文，使经典进入寺院、学校、图书馆与个人读者手中。这件物品本身就是让知识流动起来的工具。

Connections

Yang Renshan · 杨仁山创办者与校经者 · Founder and editor

Yang turned the recovery of Buddhist texts into an organized project of scholarship, production, and distribution in Nanjing.

杨仁山把佛经的搜集与复兴转化为南京一项结合学术、生产与传播的系统工程。

Modern block carvers · 当代刻工技艺传承 · Craft transmission

The blocks survive only if people continue to repair wood, read mirrored characters, and understand the rhythm of hand carving.

木版只有在有人继续修复木材、辨认反刻文字并掌握手工雕刻节奏时，才能真正保存下来。

Interesting story

The text is carved backwards · 经文是反刻的有趣之处 · An interesting detail

The reader never sees the woodblock’s characters in their final orientation. The reversed surface becomes legible only when ink and paper complete the process.

读者不会直接看到木版上的正向文字。只有当墨、纸与压力完成印刷过程，反刻的字才会变成可以阅读的经文。

Evidence

A large surviving collection · 数量庞大的木版机构证据 · Institutional evidence

The press is associated with more than 125,000 scripture blocks and eighteen large Buddhist image blocks, making preservation a living institutional task.

金陵刻经处保存一批数量超过十二万五千块的经版以及十八块大型佛像版，使保存工作成为持续运转的制度任务。

Text as material practice · 文字的物质实践从经典到木版 · From scripture to block

Every printed page records a chain of decisions about wording, calligraphy, carving, ink, paper, and correction.

每一页印本都记录了关于文字、书法、雕刻、墨、纸张与校改的一连串决定。
