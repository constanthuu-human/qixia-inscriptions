01 · Qixia Temple and Xinghuang Temple · 栖霞寺与兴皇寺

Sanlun Buddhism 三论宗

Emptiness without nihilism; the Middle Way between fixed extremes · 不落断常的中道空观

Qixia Temple landscape, associated with the Sheshan Sanlun lineage · 与摄山三论传统相关的栖霞寺景观。

Central problem and main beliefs核心问题与主要思想

Sanlun asks why people treat things, identities, and ideas as if they possessed a fixed, independent nature. Its teaching of emptiness does not say that temples, people, or actions do not exist. It says that they arise through causes, conditions, relationships, language, and perception. The Middle Way therefore rejects both eternalism and nihilism. Its two truths distinguish ordinary, conventional functioning from the ultimate absence of a permanent essence.

三论宗追问：人为什么把事物、身份和观念当成具有固定、独立本性的存在？空并不是说寺院、人物和行为不存在，而是说它们依赖因缘、关系、语言与认识而生起。中道因此同时拒绝常见与断灭两种极端。二谛把日常层面的运作，与究竟层面没有永恒自性的事实区分开来。

Nanjing temples南京寺院

Qixia Temple is the principal Nanjing centre of the Sheshan Sanlun lineage. Senglang taught the Three Treatises there during the Liang period. Xinghuang Temple was another Jiankang centre: Falang taught there, and Jizang studied under him before becoming the most influential systematizer of Chinese Sanlun thought. Xinghuang no longer survives as a major active monastery, but it remains essential to the branch’s history.

栖霞寺是南京摄山三论传统的主要中心。梁代僧朗曾在那里讲授三论。兴皇寺也是建康的重要中心：法朗在那里弘法，吉藏曾从法朗学习，后来成为中国三论思想最重要的系统化者之一。兴皇寺已不再作为南京主要活动寺院保存，但它仍是三论史不可缺少的一环。

Practices修行方式

Practice centred on studying the Three Treatises, reasoning through apparently opposite claims, teaching, debate, meditation, chanting, and monastic discipline. Debate was not only intellectual competition. Its purpose was to expose attachment to language—even attachment to the statement that everything is empty. Correct contemplation, or zhengguan, sees phenomena without turning them into permanent identities; non-acquisition, wude, prevents awakening from becoming another possession of the ego.

三论修行重视三论及其注疏的学习，通过论辩、讲说、禅观、唱诵与戒律来松动执着。论辩不只是学术竞争，而是为了显露对语言的执着——甚至包括对“万法皆空”这句话的执着。正观要求在不把现象固定为永恒身份的情况下观看世界；无得则防止把觉悟也变成自我可以占有的东西。

Differences from other branches与其他宗派的区别

Sanlun is more concerned with dismantling fixed positions than Tiantai, which builds an integrative system. Unlike Pure Land, it does not centre Amitabha’s vows and rebirth. Compared with Niutou and Fayan Chan, it gives more space to textual argument and philosophical analysis. Its distinct question is simple but demanding: can a Buddhist teaching be used without turning it into another absolute?

与构建综合体系的天台相比，三论更强调拆解固定立场。它不像净土那样以阿弥陀佛的本愿与往生为中心；与牛头、法眼禅相比，它更重视文本论证与哲学分析。它最鲜明的问题是：人能否使用佛教教法，却不把教法重新变成另一种绝对？

02 · Waguan Temple and Guangzhai Temple · 瓦官寺与光宅寺

Tiantai Buddhism 天台宗

Many teachings, one path: doctrine becomes a practice of calming and insight · 多种教法归于一乘，止观合修

A seventh-century Lotus Sutra manuscript, used here to represent Tiantai’s scriptural centre · 七世纪敦煌《法华经》写本，用以表现天台宗的经典中心。

Central problem and main beliefs核心问题与主要思想

Tiantai begins with the apparent conflict among Buddhist scriptures: one text emphasizes emptiness, another devotion, another meditation, another Buddha-nature. Instead of choosing one and discarding the rest, Tiantai interprets them as skillful means suited to different audiences. Its key framework is the Three Truths—emptiness, provisional existence, and the Middle—understood simultaneously. The Lotus Sutra becomes the clearest expression of one vehicle toward Buddhahood.

天台从佛经之间看似矛盾的问题出发：有的强调空，有的强调信仰、禅定或佛性。天台不简单地选择一种、排斥其他，而是把不同教法理解为针对不同根机的方便。它以三谛——空、假、中——作为核心框架，并认为三者应同时被理解。《法华经》则成为一乘成佛思想最清晰的表达。

Nanjing temples南京寺院

Waguan Temple is the principal Nanjing Tiantai site. Zhiyi lived and taught there for roughly eight years, lecturing on the Lotus Sutra and developing his system. Guangzhai Temple was another important location: in 587 Zhiyi lectured on the Lotus Sutra there, and Guanding recorded those lectures in the work later known as the Words and Phrases of the Lotus Sutra. The historical buildings have changed, but the locations preserve the memory of Tiantai’s formation in Jiankang.

瓦官寺是南京天台宗最重要的地点。智顗在那里居住、讲学约八年，讲授《法华经》并发展自己的思想体系。光宅寺也是重要场所：587年智顗在那里讲《法华经》，灌顶记录讲义，后来形成《法华文句》。建筑本身经历变迁，但这些地点保留了天台思想在建康形成的记忆。

Practices修行方式

Tiantai’s central practice is zhiguan, calming and contemplation. Calming settles agitation; contemplation examines thoughts and phenomena as conditioned, empty, and provisionally functioning. The two must work together. Tiantai also organized four samadhis, including seated meditation, walking meditation, combinations of walking and sitting, and practice within ordinary activity. Sutra chanting, repentance, visualization, bowing, Buddha-name recitation, and precepts were integrated rather than separated from meditation.

天台的核心修行是止观。止使散乱、躁动的心安定下来；观则观察念头与现象如何因缘而生、当体即空，同时又在世俗层面发挥作用。二者必须结合。天台还组织四种三昧，包括坐禅、行禅、行住坐卧相结合的修行，以及日常活动中的观行。诵经、忏悔、观想、礼佛、念佛和持戒，都被纳入修行整体。

Differences from other branches与其他宗派的区别

Tiantai is more constructive and integrative than Sanlun: it classifies teachings and shows how they fit together. It is broader than Pure Land’s concentrated Amitabha practice, though Tiantai can include Buddha-name recitation. It gives meditation more explicit structure than Chan, while still treating doctrine, ritual, and direct experience as one program. Its signature is the attempt to make many Buddhist paths intelligible within one complete system.

与三论相比，天台更重视建构与综合：它分类各种教法，说明它们如何彼此关联。它比以阿弥陀佛为中心的净土修行更宽广，但也可以吸收念佛。与禅宗相比，天台对禅修结构的说明更明确，同时仍把教理、仪式与亲身体验结合起来。它的特色是让多种佛教道路在一个完整体系中获得解释。

03 · Qixia Temple and Linggu Temple · 栖霞寺与灵谷寺

Pure Land Buddhism 净土法门

Faith, aspiration, and practice make liberation accessible to ordinary people · 信、愿、行使解脱道路向普通人开放

Ming dynasty Pure Land mural, used to show Amitabha devotion and the Western Pure Land imagination · 明代净土壁画，表现阿弥陀信仰与西方净土想象。

Central problem and main beliefs核心问题与主要思想

Pure Land begins with the difficulty of ordinary practice. People may have little time, limited education, illness, family duties, or trouble sustaining advanced meditation. The tradition does not therefore exclude them from liberation. It turns toward Amitabha Buddha and the Western Pure Land, where conditions are understood to be especially favourable for hearing the Dharma and progressing toward awakening. The basic pattern is faith or trust, aspiration, and practice—xin, yuan, and xing.

净土从普通人修行困难这一事实出发：人可能时间有限、教育有限，受到疾病、家庭责任或情绪牵引，也难以长期保持高深禅定。因此，净土不认为这些人被排除在解脱之外，而是引导他们归向阿弥陀佛与西方净土。那里被理解为更适合听闻佛法、继续修行的环境。其基本结构是信、愿、行。

Nanjing temples南京寺院

Pure Land should not be assigned to one exclusive Nanjing denomination. Its practices circulated through many monasteries. Qixia preserves an important early Western Pure Land triad of Amitabha, Guanyin, and Mahasthamaprapta in the Thousand Buddha Cliff landscape. Linggu Temple’s historical Wuliang Hall was dedicated to the Buddha of Infinite Life. These sites show Pure Land devotion operating within wider Buddhist institutions rather than apart from them.

净土不应被分配给南京某一座“专属宗派寺院”。相关实践在许多寺院中流传。栖霞千佛岩保存了与早期净土信仰相关的西方三圣组合：阿弥陀佛、观音与大势至。灵谷寺历史上的无量殿曾供奉无量寿佛。这些地点说明净土信仰是在更广泛的佛教制度中运作，而不是与其他传统完全分离。

Practices修行方式

The best-known practice is nianfo, remembering and reciting Amitabha’s name: Namo Amitabha Buddha. Recitation gathers attention and renews aspiration; it is not merely the production of sound. Other practices include visualizing the Pure Land, chanting Pure Land scriptures, making vows, ethical conduct, charity, merit dedication, collective temple recitation, and guidance near the end of life. A simple form does not necessarily mean an undisciplined one.

最著名的实践是念佛，即忆念并称念“南无阿弥陀佛”。念诵不只是发出声音，而是集中注意、重新确认往生愿心。其他实践还包括观想净土、诵读净土经典、发愿、持戒、布施、回向、寺院共修，以及临终时的助念。形式简单，并不意味着修行没有纪律。

Differences from other branches与其他宗派的区别

Pure Land gives greater prominence to Amitabha’s vows, devotional trust, aspiration, and Buddha-remembrance than Sanlun’s philosophical analysis or Tiantai’s comprehensive system. Chan often emphasizes recognizing mind in present experience, while Pure Land emphasizes Amitabha and rebirth in the Western Pure Land. In practice, the boundaries were porous: Chan and Tiantai monasteries could also recite Amitabha’s name.

与三论的哲学分析或天台的综合体系相比，净土更突出阿弥陀佛本愿、信心、愿生与念佛。禅宗常强调在当下认识心性，而净土强调阿弥陀佛与西方净土的往生愿景。但实践边界并不绝对：禅寺与天台寺院同样可以念阿弥陀佛。

04 · Niushou Mountain and Youqi Temple · 牛首山与幽栖寺

Niutou Chan 牛头禅

Do not turn meditation or enlightenment into another possession of the self · 不把禅定与觉悟变成自我的占有物

Mount Niushou landscape, the birthplace associated with the Niutou tradition · 与牛头传统发源记忆相关的牛首山景观。

Central problem and main beliefs核心问题与主要思想

Niutou Chan identifies a subtler form of attachment: a practitioner can become attached to meditation, holiness, or enlightenment itself. No-mind, wuxin, does not mean unconsciousness or emotional emptiness. It means that thoughts can arise without being seized as mine, my success, or my spiritual identity. Niutou teaching loosens oppositions such as sacred and ordinary, purity and impurity, motion and stillness.

牛头禅指出一种更微妙的执着：修行者也可能执着于禅定、神圣感甚至觉悟本身。无心并不是失去意识或情感空洞，而是念头可以生起，却不必立刻被抓取为“我的念头”“我的成就”或“我的宗教身份”。牛头传统松动神圣与平凡、清净与染污、动与静等对立。

Nanjing temples南京寺院

The tradition is rooted in Niushou Mountain, historically Niutou Mountain. Youqi Temple and Farong’s meditation site belong to its origin story; Foku Temple, later known as Hongjue Temple, belonged to the older mountain landscape. The modern Foding Temple and Niutou Chan cultural area preserve and commemorate this lineage, even though the contemporary buildings are not identical to the early medieval monastery.

这一传统根植于历史上称为牛头山的牛首山。幽栖寺与法融修行处属于它的起源故事；佛窟寺，后来称为宏觉寺，也属于牛首山较早的佛教景观。今天的佛顶寺与牛头禅文化区保存并纪念这一传承，但现代建筑并不等同于中古时期的寺院。

Practices修行方式

Meditation and observation of mind were central: sitting, noticing grasping as it arose, releasing attachment without forcibly suppressing thought, accepting direct instruction, and studying scriptures. Farong’s tradition also includes years of scriptural learning and lectures on the Lotus Sutra and Prajnaparamita. “No practice” does not mean doing nothing; it criticizes turning practice into an achievement owned by the ego.

禅坐与观心是核心：静坐，觉察执着如何生起，不强行压制念头而松开执着，接受老师的直接指导，并学习经典。法融传统也记载长期经论学习以及对《法华经》和般若类经典的讲说。“无修”并不是无所事事，而是批评把修行变成自我可以占有的成就。

Differences from other branches与其他宗派的区别

Niutou shares Sanlun’s concern with emptiness but relies less on formal philosophical debate. It is less systematized than Tiantai and does not centre Amitabha’s vows like Pure Land. Compared with Fayan, it is more strongly associated with no-mind and the release of mental distinctions. Its characteristic warning is that the search for enlightenment can itself become an obstacle.

牛头与三论都关心空，但不太依赖正式哲学论辩。它比天台更少体系化，也不像净土那样以阿弥陀佛本愿为中心。与法眼相比，它更突出无心与松开分别念。它最有特色的提醒是：追求觉悟本身，也可能成为觉悟的障碍。

05 · Qingliang Temple · 清凉寺

Fayan Chan 法眼宗

Awakening must appear as accurate understanding and appropriate action in each real situation · 真正的觉悟必须落实于具体情境中的理解与行动

Qingliang Temple photograph, the ancestral site associated with Fayan Wenyi · 与法眼文益及法眼宗祖庭记忆相关的南京清凉寺照片。

Central problem and main beliefs核心问题与主要思想

Fayan Chan addresses the danger of using abstract Chan language without understanding or responsible conduct. Saying that everything is empty does not make actions irrelevant; saying that Chan is beyond words does not make scriptures useless. Fayan thought connects principle and phenomena: underlying truth must be expressed through particular people, actions, objects, and situations. Doctrine is not outside the world of events.

法眼禅面对的问题是：人可能使用抽象的禅语，却没有真正理解，也没有相应的负责行为。说“万法皆空”并不意味着行为不重要；说“禅超越语言”也不意味着经典无用。法眼思想把理与事联系起来：根本真理必须在具体的人、行为、物件与情境中表现出来。教理并不在事件世界之外。

Nanjing temples南京寺院

Qingliang Temple is the central Nanjing site and remembered birthplace of the Fayan branch. Fayan Wenyi taught there under the Southern Tang, and the school took its name from his posthumous title. The temple later suffered repeated destruction and rebuilding; its modern restoration preserves an ancestral identity without making Fayan a separate modern denomination.

清凉寺是南京法眼宗的中心地点，也是后世记忆中的法眼宗祖庭。南唐时期法眼文益在那里讲学，宗派名称来自他的谥号。寺院后来经历多次毁坏与重建；今天的修复保存了祖庭身份，但法眼宗并不是一个与其他汉传佛教完全分离的现代宗派。

Practices修行方式

Fayan practice included meditation, teacher–student dialogue, scriptural study, monastic discipline, and careful attention to concrete situations. A teacher might direct a student to a sound, object, or action—not to create a mysterious puzzle, but to prevent escape into abstraction. Realization appeared in speech, teaching, daily duties, and the ability to respond appropriately to other people.

法眼修行包括禅坐、师生问答、经典学习、僧团戒律，以及对具体情境的细致关注。老师可能把学生的注意力引向声音、物件或动作，不是制造玄妙谜语，而是防止学生逃进抽象概念。觉悟要在说话、教学、日常责任和回应他人的方式中显现。

Differences from other branches与其他宗派的区别

Fayan is less centred on formal philosophical refutation than Sanlun, less classificatory than Tiantai, and less focused on Amitabha than Pure Land. Compared with Niutou, it gives more weight to doctrinal learning, community standards, and precise responses in the world of phenomena. Its signature is the demand that insight become responsible, accurate action.

法眼不像三论那样以正式哲学破执为中心，也不像天台那样重视宏大的教判体系，更不像净土那样围绕阿弥陀佛展开。与牛头相比，它更重视教理学习、僧团规范，以及在现象世界中作出准确回应。它最鲜明的要求是：见地必须转化为负责任而准确的行动。

Reasons behind the branches

The branches were not sealed denominations. They grew from the city’s political resources, textual world, teachers, landscapes, historical layers, and different religious needs.

01

A capital with resources

反复成为都城，拥有制度资源

Eastern Wu, Eastern Jin, and the Southern Dynasties repeatedly made Nanjing a political capital. Emperors, aristocrats, officials, scholars, and wealthy residents could provide land, money, protection, audiences, and materials for monasteries, images, scriptures, and pagodas.

东吴、东晋与南朝多次把南京置于都城位置。皇帝、贵族、官员、学者与富裕居民可以为寺院、造像、经书与佛塔提供土地、资金、保护、听众和材料。

02

Scriptures arrived gradually

佛教经典是逐步进入中国的

Buddhism did not arrive as one complete system. Different translators brought texts emphasizing emptiness, the Lotus Sutra, Amitabha, meditation, discipline, Buddha-nature, ritual, and repentance. Monks therefore developed different ways to interpret and organize the growing archive.

佛教并不是以一个完整体系一次性进入中国的。不同译者带来了强调空、《法华经》、阿弥陀佛、禅定、戒律、佛性、仪式与忏悔的经典。僧人因此发展出不同的解释方法，来整理不断扩大的经典世界。

03

Teachers made temple centres

高僧把寺院变成传承中心

A branch became associated with a Nanjing place when an influential teacher lived, lectured, meditated, or trained disciples there. Qixia became linked with Sanlun, Waguan with Zhiyi’s Tiantai teaching, Niushou with Farong, and Qingliang with Fayan Wenyi. These centres grew through communities, not through a government assignment of one denomination to each temple.

当一位有影响力的高僧在南京某地居住、讲学、禅修或培养弟子时，某一传统便可能与该寺院联系起来。栖霞与三论、瓦官与智顗的天台讲学、牛首与法融、清凉与法眼文益，都是这样的例子。这些中心通过师生共同体成长，并不是政府把一种宗派分配给一座寺院。

04

Urban and mountain environments

城市与山林提供不同的宗教环境

Urban temples supported public lectures, translation, debate, ceremonies, and contact with officials. Qixia and Niushou offered quieter retreats, monastic space, cliffs for image-making, and landscapes that could become sacred. The two environments were connected, but they made different styles of Buddhist life possible.

城市寺院适合公开讲学、译经、论辩、大型法会以及与官员交往。栖霞与牛首则提供更安静的退居空间、僧团场所、开凿佛像的山崖，以及可以被转化为圣境的山林。两种环境彼此相连，却让不同的佛教生活方式得以形成。

05

Different periods added new layers

不同历史时期不断增加新的层次

Kang Senghui and Zhi Qian belong to the Eastern Wu moment of translation and introduction. Southern Dynasties Jiankang developed Buddhist philosophy, court patronage, image-making, and public debate. Niutou became associated with Niushou in the seventh century, while Fayan emerged at Qingliang under the Southern Tang. New traditions accumulated without simply erasing earlier ones.

康僧会与支谦属于东吴时期的译经与佛教初传阶段。南朝建康发展出佛教哲学、宫廷赞助、造像与公共论辩。七世纪牛头禅与牛首联系起来，南唐时期法眼宗又在清凉寺形成。新的传统不断累积，却没有简单地抹去更早的传统。

06

Different traditions answered different needs

不同传统回应不同的宗教需要

Sanlun examined attachment to fixed ideas. Tiantai organized many teachings into one system. Pure Land offered Amitabha recitation and aspiration. Niutou emphasized freedom from attachment to spiritual achievement. Fayan connected insight with accurate understanding and responsible action. Their different emphases allowed several traditions to remain meaningful together.

三论检验人对固定观念的执着；天台把多种教法组织为一个体系；净土提供念佛与往生愿心；牛头强调不执着于精神成就；法眼把觉悟与准确理解、负责任的行动联系起来。不同重点使多种传统能够同时保持意义。

07

Branches were never sealed compartments

宗派从来不是完全封闭的隔间

A Chan monk might recite Amitabha’s name, study Tiantai or Sanlun texts, chant sutras, observe shared rules, join repentance ceremonies, and dedicate merit. A temple could be famous for one lineage while practising forms shared across several traditions. Nanjing Buddhism was a connected culture, not a set of isolated denominations.

一位禅僧也可能念阿弥陀佛、学习天台或三论经典、诵经、遵守共同戒律、参加忏悔法会并回向功德。一座寺院可以因某一传承而闻名，同时实践多种传统共有的方式。南京佛教是一种彼此连接的文化，而不是一组互不相干的宗派。

Nanjing Buddhism was diverse because the city repeatedly allowed new Buddhist teachings to develop without completely erasing the older ones.南京佛教之所以多元，是因为这座城市一次又一次地让新的佛教教法生长，同时没有完全抹去旧有传统。

03 · Concluding panel

How monks and laypeople formed a Buddhist community

僧人与居士如何形成佛教共同体

Nanjing temples, workshops, households, and networks · 南京寺院、工坊、家庭与网络

A temple was sustained by mutual dependence, not by monks or donors acting alone. · 寺院由彼此依赖的共同体维系，而不是由僧人或施主单独创造。

Monks and nuns maintained religious life僧人与尼众维持宗教生活

The Sangha preserved and taught Buddhist knowledge through scripture study, precepts, meditation, chanting, ceremonies, training, administration, and the care of buildings and objects. A monastery was not only a place where visitors watched rituals. It required education, food preparation, repairs, property management, and the transmission of knowledge from one generation to the next.

僧团通过学习经典、遵守戒律、禅修、唱诵、举行仪式、培养后学、管理寺院以及维护建筑和器物来保存并传授佛教知识。寺院并不只是游客观看仪式的地方，它还需要教育、备餐、修缮、财产管理，以及一代代知识的传承。

Laypeople supplied the material foundation居士提供物质基础

Land, money, food, timber, stone, metal, transportation, political protection, craft skills, and payments for images or scriptures all helped temples survive. Qixia’s foundation tradition makes the division visible: Ming Sengshao supplied a residence and land, while Fadu helped turn that property into an organized monastic community. The donor and the monk performed different but connected actions.

土地、资金、食物、木材、石料、金属、交通、政治保护、工艺技能，以及造像和刻经的费用，都帮助寺院延续。栖霞的创建传统清楚表现出这种分工：明僧绍提供居所与土地，法度则帮助把这片地产转化为有组织的僧团。施主与僧人做了不同却彼此连接的事情。

Support moved in both directions支持关系是双向的

The relationship was not simply that laypeople gave money and monks received it. Monastics offered teaching, ceremonies, moral guidance, merit dedication, memorial practices, and opportunities to hear scriptures and worship. The monastery therefore connected specialist religious labour with the emotional, intellectual, and family concerns of people outside its walls.

这种关系并不是居士给钱、僧人接受这么简单。僧人和尼众也提供讲法、仪式、道德引导、功德回向、追荐亡者的仪式，以及听闻经典和礼佛的机会。寺院因此把专业的宗教劳动，与寺院外人们的情感、思想和家庭需要连接起来。

Many kinds of people belonged to the community共同体由许多类型的人组成

A Buddhist community included monks, nuns, lay donors, translators, editors, stoneworkers, carpenters, kiln workers, calligraphers, woodblock carvers, cooks, gardeners, cleaners, attendants, pilgrims, and ordinary worshippers. Some entered written history by name; others remain visible only through an inscription, tool mark, repaired statue, printing block, or anonymous building.

佛教共同体包括僧人、尼众、施主、译者、编辑、石工、木匠、窑工、书法家、刻工、厨师、园丁、清洁者、寺院侍者、朝圣者与普通礼佛者。有些人以姓名进入文字记录，另一些人只通过碑刻、工具痕迹、修补过的造像、经版或无名建筑留下身影。

Lay Buddhists could become religious leaders居士也可以成为宗教领袖

Yang Renshan shows that lay participation could become scholarship, publishing, and institutional leadership. In 1866 he helped establish the Jinling Scriptural Press, where collecting, correcting, carving, printing, and circulating texts made Buddhist learning mobile. He remained a layman, yet his work shaped modern Buddhist education far beyond a single act of donation.

杨仁山说明，居士参与佛教可以发展为学术、出版与制度领导。1866年，他参与创办金陵刻经处，通过搜集、校勘、雕刻、印刷和流通，让佛教学问能够移动和传播。他始终是居士，但他的工作对现代佛教教育的影响远远超过一次捐赠。

Temples formed networks beyond one community寺院形成超越单一共同体的网络

Monastics travelled between temples, studied with different teachers, attended ordinations, exchanged texts, and joined wider Buddhist organizations. The citywide network matters because a temple’s life depended on more than its own gate: people, money, books, teachers, and practices moved between institutions and connected Nanjing to Buddhist communities elsewhere.

僧人会在寺院之间旅行，跟随不同老师学习，参加受戒，交换经典，并加入更大的佛教组织。城市网络之所以重要，是因为一座寺院的生命不只依赖自己的山门：人员、资金、书籍、老师与实践会在机构之间流动，把南京与其他地区的佛教共同体连接起来。

04 · Concluding panel

Why Nanjing mattered in the larger history of Chinese Buddhism

南京为何在中国佛教史上重要

Jiankang, Nanjing, and the wider Buddhist world · 建康、南京与更大的佛教世界

Nanjing became a place where Buddhism was translated, interpreted, organized, visualized, and transmitted. · 南京成为佛教被翻译、解释、组织、视觉化并继续传播的地方。

An early southern centre of Buddhism早期的南方佛教中心

As Jianye and later Jiankang, Nanjing served as the capital of Eastern Wu, Eastern Jin, and the Southern Dynasties. Its political importance brought together monks, translators, rulers, scholars, aristocratic families, and wealthy patrons. Buddhism therefore had to communicate with Chinese court culture and intellectual life rather than remain only a small community of foreign monks and translated texts.

作为建业、后来作为建康，南京曾是东吴、东晋与南朝的都城。政治地位把僧人、译者、统治者、学者、贵族家庭与富裕施主聚集在一起。佛教因此必须与中国的宫廷文化和思想生活交流，而不能只停留在少数外国僧人与译经文本的范围内。

Translated teachings became Chinese philosophy译入的教法转化为中国佛教哲学

Monks in and around Jiankang debated emptiness, karma, nirvana, Buddha-nature, meditation, discipline, and the relationship between scriptures. These debates helped turn separate translations into Chinese systems of interpretation. Qixia’s Sanlun tradition and Zhiyi’s teaching at Waguan show Nanjing participating directly in the formation of major Chinese Buddhist thought.

建康及其周边的僧人讨论空、业、涅槃、佛性、禅定、戒律，以及不同经典之间的关系。这些讨论帮助佛教把彼此分散的译本转化为中国式的解释体系。栖霞的三论传统与智顗在瓦官寺的讲学，都说明南京直接参与了中国佛教思想的形成。

Many traditions found important centres here多种传统在这里找到重要中心

Qixia became associated with Sanlun, Waguan with Tiantai, Niushou with Niutou Chan, and Qingliang with Fayan Chan. Pure Land devotion also appeared within wider Nanjing institutions. These traditions did not merely receive ideas from elsewhere; teachers, students, texts, and practices reorganized Buddhism in ways that could travel beyond the city.

栖霞与三论、瓦官与天台、牛首与牛头禅、清凉与法眼宗分别建立了重要联系。净土信仰也在南京更广泛的佛教机构中出现。这些传统并不是被动接受外来思想，而是通过老师、学生、经典与实践重新组织佛教，并使其能够向城市之外传播。

Buddhism and political power met here佛教与政治权力在这里相遇

Capital status gave rulers and officials opportunities to build temples, sponsor translations and lectures, regulate monasteries, commission images, and use Buddhist language to express virtue and legitimacy. Emperor Wu of Liang is a major example. Yet the same connection could bring regulation, relocation, destruction, and rebuilding. Nanjing shows both the opportunities and risks of close ties between religion and government.

都城地位使统治者与官员能够修建寺院、赞助译经与讲学、管理僧团、制作造像，并用佛教语言表达德性与合法性。梁武帝就是重要例子。但这种关系也可能带来管制、迁移、毁坏与重建。南京同时展示了宗教与政府密切联系所带来的机会与风险。

Art and sacred landscapes carried the teachings艺术与圣境承载佛教教法

Cliff carvings, bronze images, relic pagodas, glazed architecture, temple plans, tiles, inscriptions, and calligraphy made Buddhist ideas visible. Qixia’s Thousand Buddha Cliff placed Buddhist forms within a Jiangnan mountain landscape. Nanjing therefore helped transform Buddhism from translated teaching into a physical and visual culture.

摩崖造像、铜像、舍利塔、琉璃建筑、寺院布局、瓦当、碑刻与书法，都使佛教思想变得可见。栖霞千佛岩把佛教造像放入江南山林景观之中。南京因此帮助佛教从译入的教法，转化为一种物质而视觉的文化。

Ideas and texts travelled beyond the city思想与经典从南京走向更远的地方

Teachers trained disciples who travelled, commentaries circulated through temple networks, and lineages associated with Nanjing entered wider East Asian histories. The Jinling Scriptural Press created another route of influence: its woodblocks made corrected Buddhist texts reproducible, allowing learning to move beyond a single temple, teacher, or generation.

老师培养的弟子会继续旅行，注疏通过寺院网络流通，与南京有关的传承也进入更广阔的东亚历史。金陵刻经处又创造了另一条影响路径：经版使校订后的佛教文本可以反复印刷，让知识超越一座寺院、一位老师或一代人继续移动。

Ancient history continues through modern revival古代历史通过现代复兴延续

Nanjing’s Buddhist importance did not end with the medieval period. The Jinling Scriptural Press supported modern Buddhist publishing and learning, while temples, academies, museums, and universities continue to study and transmit the city’s heritage. Nanjing offers an unusual sequence: ancient translation and philosophy, medieval schools and art, modern printing, and contemporary research.

南京的佛教重要性并没有在中古时期结束。金陵刻经处支持了近现代佛教出版与学习，寺院、佛学院、博物馆与大学也继续研究和传播这座城市的佛教遗产。南京呈现出少见的连续链条：古代译经与哲学、中古宗派与艺术、近现代印刷，以及当代研究。
