Images · Form · Meaning

Buddhist images

Six Buddhist images and image traditions from Nanjing show how religious meaning entered public life through form, place, ritual, and memory.

How a Buddhist image makes meaning visible

Form carries a history.

A Buddhist image is not just a picture of a sacred figure. It is made meaningful by date, material, placement, ritual use, stories, and the people who encounter it.

01 · 527 · Liang Datong 1 · Gilt bronze image · 铜鎏金造像

The dated Liang bronze Buddha 南朝梁纪年铜佛像

Nanjing Municipal Museum · 南京市博物馆

A small image makes a date, a ritual gesture, and a Buddhist community visible at once.

Museum photograph from the record of the 527 Liang bronze image · 527年南朝梁铜佛像的博物馆图像。

Historical story

Excavated near Deji Square in Nanjing, this small bronze image carries a date that anchors it in the first year of Liang Datong, 527. Its value is not only its size or surface. The inscription places an image, a donor world, and a moment in the religious history of Jiankang into one portable object.

这件小型铜像出土于南京德基广场附近，所带纪年把它确定在梁大同元年，即527年。它的价值不只在于尺寸或表面装饰。铭文把一尊佛像、一个施主世界和建康佛教史中的某一时刻，放进同一件可以被供奉与移动的器物中。

What the image shows 可见形制

A central Buddha with attendants佛与胁侍

The composition is read as a central Buddha accompanied by two bodhisattvas, with smaller Buddha figures worked into the flame-shaped halo. The arrangement makes a single image into a compact Buddhist assembly.

这组造像通常被读作中央佛像与两位菩萨相伴，火焰形背光上还刻有小型佛像。一个小小的造像因此容纳了一组佛教会众。

The date is part of the object纪年就是器物的一部分

The inscription naming the first year of Datong is unusually important: it turns the bronze from an undated devotional image into a securely placed historical witness.

刻有“大同元年”的铭文尤其重要：它把这件原本可能无明确年代的供像，转化为一件可以放入具体历史时间的见证物。

Gesture and reading 手势与解读

The raised right hand右手上举

The raised right hand can be read as a reassurance gesture, often called abhaya or 施无畏印. The left hand is less secure in the surviving photograph; it should not be assigned a precise mudra simply because a familiar pair is expected.

右手上举可以理解为安慰、无畏的手势，通常称为施无畏印。现存图像中左手细节并不充分，不应因为常见的手印组合而强行给它指定一个确定名称。

Why it matters 它为什么重要

Portable devotion可以移动的信仰

Unlike a cliff or a temple hall, a small bronze could travel with a household, be installed in a local shrine, or be offered to a monastery. Its scale reveals another route by which Buddhism became present.

与山崖或寺院大殿不同，小型铜像可以随家族移动，安置在小型佛堂，或奉献给寺院。它的尺寸显示了佛教进入社会生活的另一条路径。

02 · Late fifth century tradition · Southern Qi / Liang site · Rock-cut triad · 石窟三尊像

The Western Pure Land triad at Qixia 栖霞山西方三圣

Qixia Mountain, Thousand Buddha Cliff · 栖霞山千佛岩

Three figures turn a cliff into a place where a community could imagine a Buddha-land beyond the mountain.

Site photograph of the surviving niche field; it is not a close-up of the triad itself · 千佛岩现存佛龛群遗址照片，并非西方三圣的单独近景。

Historical story

The Western Pure Land triad is identified as Amitabha with Guanyin and Mahasthamaprapta. At Qixia, the important historical fact is not only the names of the three figures, but the way a group of images was cut into a living mountain sanctuary. The cliff accumulated patrons, repairs, weathering, and new acts of looking over many centuries.

西方三圣通常指阿弥陀佛、观音菩萨与大势至菩萨。在栖霞，重要的历史事实不只是三尊像的名称，还在于这一组佛像如何被开凿进一座持续使用的山林圣境。历代施主、修补、风化和观看行为，共同累积成了今天的山崖。

The group 三尊组合

Amitabha at the center中央的阿弥陀佛

The central Buddha gives the group its Pure Land orientation. The two flanking bodhisattvas make the image a triad rather than an isolated Buddha portrait.

中央佛像使这组造像具有净土方向。两侧胁侍菩萨把它构成三尊组合，而不是一幅孤立的佛像。

A reading stronger than a hand gesture组合比手印更可靠

Weathering and later surface changes make individual mudras difficult to read from a general site photograph. The triad’s group identity is more secure than a detailed claim about each hand.

风化与后来的表面变化使我们难以从遗址全景中准确辨认每一只手的手印。三尊组合的身份，比对每个手势作细密判断更可靠。

Stone and landscape 石头与山林

The cliff is an active shrine山崖是一座持续生成的佛堂

The figures are inseparable from the rock face, paths, vegetation, and the movement of visitors. Worship happened through approaching, seeing, touching, lighting incense, and remembering.

造像不能脱离岩壁、山路、植物与访客的移动来理解。礼拜通过接近、观看、触摸、焚香与记忆发生。

A careful historical reading 谨慎的历史阅读

Not one untouched moment不是一个未经变化的时刻

The site preserves a layered history. The late-fifth-century foundation tradition is important, but the present surface also carries later interventions and losses.

这一遗址保存的是层累的历史。五世纪晚期的创建传统很重要，但今天看到的表面也包含后世修整与损失。

03 · Temple tradition · Northern Wei attribution · Stone niche and triad · 石龛与三圣像

The Huayan triad at Pilu Temple 毗卢寺华严三圣

Pilu Temple, Nanjing · 南京毗卢寺

The image teaches the universe through a relationship: Vairocana at the center, wisdom and practice at either side.

Temple-context photograph; the exact stone niche and hand details require a dedicated close-up · 毗卢寺寺院环境照片；石龛原像与手势需要专门近景才能判断。

Historical story

Pilu Temple’s Huayan triad is read as Vairocana Buddha with Manjusri and Samantabhadra. The tradition gives the viewer a map of Buddhist knowledge: the universal Buddha in the middle, wisdom and practice in relation. Because the available photograph shows the temple context rather than the niche close-up, this record distinguishes the interpretive tradition from what can be read directly from the pixels.

毗卢寺华严三圣通常被理解为毗卢遮那佛、文殊菩萨与普贤菩萨。这个组合为观者提供了一张佛教知识地图：中央是遍照一切的佛，两侧分别关联智慧与实践。由于目前可用的照片展示的是寺院环境而非石龛近景，本记录把寺院的解释传统与照片本身能够直接显示的内容分开。

The Huayan relationship 华严关系

Vairocana at the center毗卢遮那佛居中

The central figure represents the all-encompassing Buddha of the Huayan world, while Manjusri and Samantabhadra embody wisdom and practice in the standard reading of the triad.

中央主尊代表华严世界中遍照一切的佛；按照通常的组合解释，文殊与普贤分别关联智慧与实践。

A doctrine made spatial把教义转化为空间

The three figures make an abstract teaching visible as a balanced arrangement. Viewers can approach a relationship rather than memorize a definition.

三尊像把抽象教义转化为平衡的空间关系。观者面对的是一个可以接近、观看的关系，而不只是一个需要背诵的定义。

What the photograph can show 照片能够显示什么

Temple setting, not exact gesture寺院环境，而非具体手印

The current open photograph supports the location and architectural context. It does not support a confident identification of the niche’s damaged attributes or mudras.

目前找到的开放照片可以证明寺院所在与建筑环境，但不足以确定石龛中残损的持物或手印。

Why the distinction matters 区分的意义

Interpretation needs a surface解释必须落在可见表面上

A named tradition can guide research, but it should not replace looking. A future close-up can add details; until then, the restrained reading is the more honest one.

已有名称可以引导研究，但不能替代观看。将来若有石龛近景，可以补充细节；在此之前，保持克制的阅读更诚实。

04 · Temple tradition · modern ritual setting · Temple image · 寺院观音像

The reverse-seated Guanyin at Jiming Temple 鸡鸣寺倒坐观音

Jiming Temple, Nanjing · 南京鸡鸣寺

A change of direction turns compassion into a question: who is the image facing, and who has failed to turn back?

Temple exterior/context photograph; the interior Guanyin image is not visible here · 鸡鸣寺建筑环境照片；照片中看不到殿内倒坐观音像。

Historical story

Jiming Temple’s Guanyin is popularly called reverse-seated because the image faces north rather than toward the main approach. The temple couplet asks why the Bodhisattva sits turned away and answers that living beings refuse to turn back. The story is compelling because it makes orientation into a moral lesson, while the exact crown, objects, and hand gesture still require a close photograph of the statue itself.

鸡鸣寺观音像因为面向北方、没有朝向通常的正门方向，而被称为“倒坐观音”。寺中楹联问“菩萨为何倒坐”，又答“叹众生不肯回头”。这个故事之所以有力量，是因为它把朝向变成道德寓言；但观音的具体冠饰、持物与手印，仍需雕像近景才能判断。

The direction 朝向

Facing the other way面向另一方

The defining feature in the temple tradition is orientation: the image faces north, toward the city and its living public, rather than simply presenting its back as a damaged or accidental view.

寺院传统中最明确的特征是朝向：观音面向北方，朝向城市与现实中的众生，而不是因为损坏或偶然观看才显得背对观者。

Compassion as a turn 把慈悲理解为回头

A visual sermon一场无言的说法

The image’s unusual direction creates a small drama. The viewer is asked to imagine that the problem lies not in the Bodhisattva’s attention, but in human beings’ refusal to turn toward awakening.

不寻常的朝向制造了一个小型戏剧：观者被邀请去想象，问题不在菩萨是否注视众生，而在众生是否愿意转向觉悟。

What remains unseen 仍然不可见的部分

Do not invent the details不虚构细节

An exterior temple photograph cannot identify the statue’s mudra, attributes, or surface repairs. Those details should be added only when a clear interior image or temple record makes them visible.

寺院外景照片不能确定观音像的手印、持物或表面修补。只有在清晰的殿内照片或寺院记录能够显示这些细节时，才应补充解释。

05 · Ming dynasty · Grand Bao’en Temple · Glazed architectural component · 琉璃建筑构件

A flying celestial on a glazed arch 大报恩寺飞天纹琉璃构件

Grand Bao’en Temple glazed architecture · 大报恩寺琉璃建筑

A celestial figure shows how movement, music, and devotion could be built into the skin of a monument.

Open-licensed photograph of a surviving glazed arch component · 现存琉璃拱门构件的开放许可照片。

Historical story

The Grand Bao’en Temple complex used glazed architectural parts to make a building shine with color and movement. The flying celestial is not a Buddha, bodhisattva, or monk. It belongs to a decorative and musical world around the sacred center: a figure who carries the atmosphere of a Buddhist paradise into the structure itself.

大报恩寺建筑群以琉璃构件制造色彩与运动感。飞天不是佛、菩萨或僧人，而是围绕神圣中心展开的装饰与音乐世界中的形象：它把佛教净土般的氛围带入建筑本身。

Read the figure 阅读飞天

A body in motion处于运动中的身体

Flowing ribbons and an airborne posture make the figure appear to move across the glazed surface. The point is not realistic anatomy; it is the visual promise of effortless motion.

飘带与飞行姿态使人物像是在琉璃表面掠过。这里的重点不是写实的人体比例，而是无重力、无阻碍的运动感。

Joined hands, musical presence合掌与音乐性

The figure’s joined hands and elongated body suggest ritual presence and celestial performance. If a small attribute is obscured, the larger role remains legible from the decorative program.

人物合掌与舒展的身体暗示仪式性存在与天界表演。即使细小持物已经模糊，从整体装饰语境中仍能看出它的角色。

Material and monument 材料与建筑

Religion in the surface建筑表面的宗教性

The figure did not stand alone in a shrine. It was part of a larger glazed system, where color, repetition, light, and height made imperial Buddhist devotion visible from a distance.

飞天并不是独立供奉在小龛中的形象，而是更大琉璃系统的一部分。色彩、重复、光线与高度，使帝国佛教的崇敬可以从远处被看见。

A useful distinction 一个重要区分

Not a portrait of a monk不是僧人肖像

Its religious role is symbolic and decorative. Calling it a monk would erase the visual vocabulary of Buddhist celestial beings that the builders chose for the monument.

它的宗教角色是象征性和装饰性的。若把它称为僧人，就会抹去建筑营造者选择的佛教天界形象语言。

06 · Contemporary · Niushou Mountain Buddhist Culture Park · Modern full-copper image · 当代全铜造像

The reclining Buddha at Niushou 牛首山释迦牟尼卧像

Rulian Theatre, Niushou Mountain · 牛首山如莲剧场

The reclining body makes nirvana into a slow, public encounter rather than a distant doctrinal word.

Official Niushou Mountain image for the Rulian Theatre setting; the reclining image is a modern work, not an ancient sculpture · 牛首山官方如莲剧场场景图；卧像为当代作品，并非古代造像。

Historical story

Niushou Mountain describes the image as a 7.5-metre full-copper reclining Shakyamuni with a pale stone-like surface. It slowly rotates through 360 degrees, presenting the Buddha’s final nirvana as a calm movement through space. The work belongs to contemporary religious tourism and interpretation, but its pose draws on a very old Buddhist visual language.

牛首山资料将这尊释迦牟尼卧像描述为全长7.5米的全铜造像，表面仿作汉白玉质感。它可以缓慢旋转360度，以空间中的平静运动表现佛陀最后的涅槃。作品属于当代宗教景观与展示，但它的卧姿承接了非常古老的佛教图像语言。

Posture 姿态

Nirvana in repose安详的涅槃

The reclining body is not simply sleeping. In Buddhist art, the pose refers to the Buddha’s final passing into nirvana, a moment represented through stillness rather than dramatic action.

卧姿并不只是睡眠。在佛教艺术中，它指向佛陀最后进入涅槃的时刻；这种时刻通过安静而非剧烈动作来表现。

Material and movement 材料与运动

Copper made to look like stone仿石质感的铜

The full-copper body and pale surface show a modern desire to combine durability with the visual calm of white stone. Its slow rotation turns viewing into a measured pilgrimage around the image.

全铜身体与浅色表面显示出当代设计把耐久材料与白石般的宁静视觉结合起来的愿望。缓慢旋转则把观看变成围绕造像展开的有节奏的行走。

Why a modern image belongs here 为什么当代造像也属于这里

An old story in a new setting古老故事的新场景

The object lets visitors compare two kinds of Buddhist memory: ancient forms preserved in stone and a present-day image built to make the same story physically accessible to a new public.

这件作品让观者比较两种佛教记忆：一种是保存在古代石刻中的形式，另一种是为当代公众重新建造、让同一故事可以被身体性接近的图像。
