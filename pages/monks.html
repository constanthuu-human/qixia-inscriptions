Monks in the Jiankang story

Ten monks whose lives connect Jiankang to translation, doctrine, travel, philosophy, Chan, and the rebuilding of Buddhist institutions.

A connected cast

Different monks solved different problems.

No single monk created Nanjing Buddhism. These profiles follow the people who translated, taught, travelled, organized, recorded, protected, and revived Buddhist institutions.

Dates and biographies are unevenly documented. Where a story comes from later tradition, the record keeps that uncertainty visible.

01 · d. 280 · Eastern Wu · Monk, translator, and ritual specialist

Kang Senghui 康僧会

Jianchu Temple · 建初寺

Later traditional representation · 后世传统画像

Temple connections

Jianchu Temple · 建初寺居住与弘法 · Resided and taught

The received biography places Kang Senghui at Jianchu Temple after Sun Quan supported the community. It is the clearest Nanjing temple association in his life.

传统传记记载，孙权支持僧会之后，他以建初寺为活动中心。这是他在南京最明确的寺院联系。

The first hut · 初立茅茨早期道场 · Early practice space

Before the court-backed monastery, the story begins with a simple hut, an image, and a small community. This was a practice space, not yet the later institutional complex.

在获得朝廷支持之前，故事从一间茅屋、一尊佛像和一个小型共同体开始。那是早期修行空间，还不是后来的寺院建筑群。

Deeds

Made Buddhism public · 使佛教进入公共空间Concrete action · 具体行动

He addressed the Wu court, established a stable Buddhist institution, translated works such as the Liudu ji jing, and helped make Buddhist chanting and meditation intelligible in the capital.

他面向吴廷弘法，建立稳定的佛教机构，译出《六度集经》等作品，也让佛教唱诵与禅修在都城中变得可以理解。

Built an institution · 建立制度空间From hut to monastery · 从茅屋到寺院

His importance lies in connecting preaching, relic worship, translation, and a resident community rather than treating them as separate activities.

他的历史意义在于把弘法、舍利崇拜、译经和常住僧团连接起来，而不是把它们看成彼此分离的活动。

Titles and nicknames

Pioneer of Jiangnan Buddhism · 江南佛教开拓者Later historical description · 后世历史称述

This is a later honorific description, not a formal office awarded during his lifetime.

这是后世的尊称，并不是他生前获得的正式官职。

Translator and founder · 译经僧与寺院创建者Historical position · 历史定位

These labels summarize his remembered work: translation and the creation of a durable Buddhist base in Jianye.

这些称谓概括了他的历史形象：译经，以及在建业建立持久的佛教基地。

Masters and disciples

Master · 师承Not securely documented · 史料未明

The surviving biography does not securely identify a famous personal master for Kang Senghui.

现存传记没有可靠地记载一位明确的著名师父。

Community · 法属与同道Religious network · 宗教网络

The biography mentions members of his religious community, but it does not establish a secure list of famous personal disciples.

传记提到他的法属和宗教共同体成员，但没有确定地列出著名亲传弟子。

Interesting things

The relic test · 舍利试验Religious biography · 宗教传记

The Gaoseng zhuan says Sun Quan demanded proof. After twenty-one days of prayer, a relic allegedly appeared in a bronze vessel and resisted being broken. The miracle is a religious narrative, not a verifiable court transcript.

《高僧传》说孙权要求证明佛力。祈求二十一日后，传说有舍利出现在铜瓶中，击之不坏。这是宗教叙事，不是可以核验的吴廷现场记录。

Why the story matters · 故事为何重要Historical reading · 历史解读

The story explains why royal support for the first monastery could be remembered as legitimate: the foreign religion had answered the court in a language the court respected.

这个故事解释了为什么后世可以把最早寺院的皇家支持记为正当：外来的宗教以朝廷能够接受的方式回应了朝廷。

02 · c. 355–434 · Eastern Jin–Liu Song · Monk, philosopher, and interpreter of Buddha-nature

Zhu Daosheng 竺道生

Qingyuan / Longguang Temple · 青园寺／龙光寺

Associated landscape; no secure portrait · 相关传说景观，未见可靠肖像

Temple connections

Qingyuan / Longguang Temple · 青园寺／龙光寺Principal Jiankang residence · 建康主要居所

His principal later residence in Jiankang was Qingyuan Temple, later associated with the name Longguang Temple. This is a residence and teaching connection.

他在建康后期的主要居所是青园寺，后来与龙光寺之名相联系。这是居住与讲学的寺院联系。

Waguan Temple · 瓦官寺Early study environment · 早期学习环境

As a child, he encountered his first teacher Zhu Fatai in the Buddhist environment of Waguan Temple. This should be labeled studied or encountered—not his principal residence.

少年时期，他在瓦官寺的佛教环境中接触到最初的老师竺法汰。这里应标为学习或接触地，而不是他的主要居所。

Lushan · 庐山Study network · 学习网络

He was associated with the community connected with Huiyuan at Lushan. The connection indicates study and intellectual formation, not a claim that he resided there permanently.

他与庐山慧远一系的佛教共同体有关。这表示学习与思想形成，不表示他在那里长期常住。

Deeds

Read against the received text · 重新解释经典Critical reading · 批判性阅读

Daosheng argued that even an icchantika, a person apparently without spiritual goodness, could eventually attain Buddhahood. He reached this position while working with an incomplete Chinese translation of the Nirvana tradition.

道生主张，即使是一阐提——被认为没有善根的人——也最终可以成佛。他是在中文《涅槃》译本尚不完整时提出这一判断的。

Defended sudden enlightenment · 维护顿悟思想Doctrinal action · 教义行动

He argued that true awakening is not simply the accumulation of many partial awakenings. His position made the question of how enlightenment occurs a central philosophical problem.

他认为真正的觉悟并不是许多局部觉悟的简单累积。他让“觉悟如何发生”成为重要的哲学问题。

Titles and nicknames

Shenggong · 生公Respectful name · 尊称

Shenggong is his best-known respectful name. It is not the name of a temple office.

“生公”是他最著名的尊称，不是寺院职务名称。

One of Kumārajīva's outstanding disciples · 鸠摩罗什门下名僧Historical position · 历史定位

He is remembered among the outstanding students associated with Kumārajīva's translation and teaching circle.

他被记为鸠摩罗什译经与讲学圈中的重要弟子之一。

Masters and disciples

Zhu Fatai · 竺法汰First teacher · 初师

Zhu Fatai is identified as his first teacher in the early Buddhist environment around Waguan Temple.

竺法汰被记为他在瓦官寺佛教环境中的最初老师。

Kumārajīva · 鸠摩罗什Major intellectual master · 重要思想老师

Daosheng later studied with Kumārajīva, whose translation circle gave him access to major Indian Buddhist texts and debates.

道生后来从鸠摩罗什学习，进入其译经圈，接触重要的印度佛教经典与论争。

Huiyuan's circle · 慧远一系Associated intellectual network · 相关思想网络

His association with Huiyuan's Lushan community should be described as an intellectual connection, not automatically as a formal master–disciple relationship.

他与慧远庐山共同体的联系应表述为思想网络关系，不能自动写成正式师徒关系。

Interesting things

The nodding stones · 顽石点头Later legend · 后世传说

The later story says that rejected by people, he lectured to stones at Tiger Hill and the stones nodded. The stones are legendary; the doctrinal controversy is historically more important.

后世传说，他被人排斥后在虎丘对石头讲《涅槃经》，石头竟点头。石头故事属于传说；真正重要的是他的教义争论。

An idea before its evidence · 证据到来之前的思想Student question · 给学生的问题

His story turns an ordinary scholarly action—reading a difficult text carefully—into a question about whether a minority interpretation can be right before later evidence arrives.

他的故事把一个普通的学术行为——仔细阅读难懂的经典——变成一个问题：在新证据到来之前，少数解释是否可能是正确的？

03 · c. 337–422 · Eastern Jin–Liu Song · Pilgrim, translator, and collector of monastic law

Faxian 法显

Daochang Temple · 道场寺

Later statue; modern photograph · 后世塑像，现代摄影

Temple connections

Daochang Temple · 道场寺Translation and collaboration · 译经与合作

After returning from South and Southeast Asia, Faxian came to Jiankang and worked at Daochang Temple with Buddhabhadra. The temple is best presented as a translation and collaboration base.

从南亚和东南亚返回后，法显来到建康，在道场寺与佛陀跋陀罗合作。这里最好表述为译经与合作基地。

The journey abroad · 海外求法路线Studied and collected abroad · 海外学习与搜集

His most important study was not at a Nanjing temple. He travelled through Central Asia, India, and Sri Lanka to seek manuscripts and monastic rules.

他最重要的学习并不发生在南京寺院。他经过中亚、印度和斯里兰卡，寻找经本与戒律。

No invented Nanjing residenceEvidence boundary · 证据边界

The evidence supports his post-return association with Daochang Temple; it should not be expanded into a claim that every stage of his life was based in Jiankang.

史料支持他回国后与道场寺的联系，但不能扩展成他一生各阶段都以建康为居所。

Deeds

Went to find missing Vinaya texts · 寻找残缺戒律A journey with a purpose · 有明确目的的旅行

He left China because Chinese monasteries lacked complete versions of important Vinaya texts. The pilgrimage was an institutional research project as well as a religious journey.

他因中国寺院缺少完整的重要戒律文本而出发。这次旅行既是宗教朝圣，也是为寺院寻找制度文本的研究行动。

Returned and translated · 回国后译经Daochang Temple work · 道场寺工作

At Daochang Temple he collaborated with Buddhabhadra on texts including the Mahāsāṃghika Vinaya, making rules for ordination and communal life more available to Chinese monasteries.

在道场寺，他与佛陀跋陀罗合作翻译包括《摩诃僧祇律》在内的文本，使授戒与僧团生活的戒律更容易为中国寺院使用。

Recorded what he saw · 记录所见Foguo ji · 《佛国记》

His travel record preserved observations about Buddhist sites, geography, institutions, and social life across early fifth-century Asia.

他的旅行记录保存了关于五世纪初亚洲佛教圣地、地理、制度与社会生活的观察。

Titles and nicknames

The seeker of the law · 求法僧Religious role · 宗教角色

This is a descriptive title for a monk who travelled abroad to obtain scriptures and discipline texts, not an imperial office.

这是对出国寻求经律的僧人的描述性称谓，不是帝国官职。

Pilgrim and translator · 朝圣者与译经僧Historical position · 历史定位

His historical identity joins travel, textual collection, translation, and institutional reform.

他的历史身份把旅行、文本搜集、翻译和寺院制度建设结合在一起。

Masters and disciples

Early master · 早期师承Not securely identified · 史料未明

The standard biography does not securely preserve a famous personal master for Faxian.

标准传记没有可靠保存一位著名的个人师父。

Buddhabhadra · 佛陀跋陀罗Translation collaborator · 译经合作者

Buddhabhadra was his principal collaborator at Daochang Temple. This was a working translation relationship, not simply a claim that one was the other's ordinary temple master.

佛陀跋陀罗是他在道场寺的主要合作译者。这是工作性的译经关系，不能简单写成普通寺院师徒关系。

The travelling group · 同行求法者Shared mission · 共同使命

He began the journey with companions including Huijing, Daozheng, Huiying, and Huiwei, according to the opening of the Faxian biography.

根据《法显传》开头，他与慧景、道整、慧应、慧嵬等同行求法。

Interesting things

The storm at sea · 海上风暴Travel narrative · 旅行叙事

Later accounts say frightened passengers considered throwing him overboard during a storm, until another passenger warned them that harming a respected monk could bring consequences. The episode shows the danger and moral imagination of the journey.

后世记载，海上遇风暴时，惊恐的乘客曾想把他扔下船，另一个乘客警告说伤害高僧可能招致报应。这个故事表现了旅途的危险与宗教道德想象。

Why risk a life for a rulebook? · 为何为戒律冒险Student question · 给学生的问题

Faxian's story makes a monastic rulebook feel material: without it, ordination, communal administration, and daily practice could not be organized with confidence.

法显的故事让戒律文本变得具体：没有它，授戒、僧团管理和日常修行就难以有可靠的制度依据。

04 · 445–518 · Southern Liang · Vinaya specialist, bibliographer, and archivist

Sengyou 僧祐

Dinglin / Jianchu Temple · 定林寺／建初寺

Associated temple landscape; no secure portrait · 相关寺院景观，未见可靠肖像

Temple connections

Dinglin Temple · 定林寺Studied and worked there · 在此学习与工作

Sengyou trained at Dinglin Temple and made it one of the principal bases of his disciplinary and bibliographic work. This is a documented study and institutional connection.

僧祐在定林寺受学，并把这里作为戒律与佛教文献工作的主要基地之一。这是有史料支持的学习与制度联系。

Jianchu Temple · 建初寺Established a collection there · 在此建立经藏

He also established a scripture collection at Jianchu Temple and is remembered as having died there. The record shows institutional work, not simply a temporary visit.

他又在建初寺建立经藏，并被记载卒于此处。史料显示的是制度性工作，而不只是短暂到访。

Deeds

Catalogued Buddhist knowledge · 编目佛教知识Chu sanzang ji ji · 《出三藏记集》

He compiled the Chu sanzang ji ji, the earliest surviving comprehensive catalogue of Chinese Buddhist translations. It records titles, translators, prefaces, doubtful works, and texts that later disappeared.

他编纂《出三藏记集》，这是现存最早的完整中国佛教译经目录之一。书中记录经名、译者、序文、疑伪作品以及后来失传的文本。

Built an archive · 建立文献机构Material preservation · 文献保存

He compared manuscripts, organized repositories, repaired temples, and directed donations toward copying and preserving texts. His concrete achievement was making Buddhist memory searchable and transmissible.

他校勘写本、整理经藏、修缮寺院，并把捐赠用于抄写与保存文本。他的具体成就是让佛教记忆能够被查找与传递。

Titles and nicknames

Vinaya Master · 律师A learned designation · 学术与戒律称谓

The title identifies his expertise in monastic discipline; it is not a civil office or a claim that he founded every institution associated with him.

这个称谓说明他精通僧团戒律，并不是世俗官职，也不意味着他创建了所有与他有关的寺院。

Buddhist bibliographer · 佛教目录学家Historical position · 历史定位

This modern description captures the labor for which his surviving works are most valuable: identifying, ordering, and evaluating texts.

这个现代称呼概括了他最有史料价值的工作：辨认、整理和评估佛教文本。

Masters and disciples

Fada · 法达Teacher at Dinglin · 定林寺老师

The traditional biography connects his early Vinaya training with Fada at Dinglin Temple.

传统传记把他早期的戒律学习与定林寺的法达联系起来。

Faying · 法颖 and studentsVinaya network · 律学网络

Faying was an important Vinaya teacher. Sengyou’s students included Zhengdu, Baochang, Zhizang, and Mingche; the network mattered as much as any single successor.

法颖是重要的律学老师。僧祐的学生包括正度、宝唱、智藏、明彻等；这个网络本身和某一位继承人同样重要。

Interesting things

The escape to the monastery · 逃入寺院A life-changing decision · 改变人生的决定

A traditional account says his family tried to arrange a marriage when he was young, so he escaped to Dinglin Temple and entered religious life. The story is preserved in biography and should be read as memory as well as fact.

传统记载说，他年轻时家人曾为他安排婚事，于是他逃到定林寺出家。这个故事保存在传记中，既要读作记忆，也要注意其史料性质。

A monk as an editor · 僧人也是编辑Why it matters · 故事为何重要

Sengyou’s greatness was patient: locating damaged texts, identifying origins, and preserving information other readers might overlook.

僧祐的伟大在于耐心：寻找残缺文本、辨认来源，并保存其他读者可能忽略的信息。

05 · 418–514 · Southern Liang · Charismatic ascetic and court religious figure

Baozhi 宝志

Daolin Temple · 道林寺

Later traditional representation, printed c. 1600 · 后世传统画像，约十七世纪刻本

Temple connections

Daolin Temple · 道林寺Entered monastic life there · 在此出家

The traditional record places Baozhi’s entry into monastic life under Sengjian at Daolin Temple in the Zhongshan area. This is his early institutional connection, not proof that he spent his entire career there.

传统记载把宝志出家之事放在钟山一带的道林寺，并说他师从僧俭。这是他的早期制度联系，并不能证明他一生都住在那里。

Kaishan Jingshe · 开善精舍Commemorated after death · 身后纪念寺院

Emperor Wu built Kaishan Jingshe in his memory after his death. It should be labeled a memorial institution, not a temple where Baozhi resided during his lifetime.

梁武帝在他去世后建开善精舍以作纪念。这里应标注为身后纪念机构，而不是宝志生前居住的寺院。

Deeds

Moved between people and court · 往来民间与宫廷Charismatic authority · 魅力型宗教权威

Baozhi’s authority came through encounters with ordinary residents, monks, officials, and the Liang court rather than through one stable lecture institution.

宝志的权威来自他与普通居民、僧人、官员和梁廷的交往，而不是来自一个固定的讲学机构。

Shaped imperial memory · 影响帝王记忆Religious influence · 宗教影响

His relationship with Emperor Wu helped make the charismatic monk a figure who could advise, challenge, or morally unsettle a ruler.

他与梁武帝的关系，使具有魅力的僧人能够成为劝诫、挑战甚至在道德上震动统治者的人物。

Titles and nicknames

Zhigong / Baogong · 志公／宝公Popular names · 民间称呼

These familiar names reflect the public intimacy of his reputation and are not formal offices.

这些亲切称呼体现了他在民间声望中的亲近感，并不是正式官职。

Baozhi Dashi · 宝志大士Later honorific · 后世尊称

Dashi, ‘great being,’ is a later religious honorific. The title records reverence rather than a contemporary appointment.

“大士”是后世宗教尊称。这个称谓记录的是崇敬，而不是当时的任命。

Masters and disciples

Sengjian · 僧俭Early master · 早期师父

The traditional biography identifies Sengjian of Daolin Temple as Baozhi’s early master.

传统传记把道林寺的僧俭记为宝志的早期师父。

Emperor Wu · 梁武帝Political-religious relationship · 政教关系

The emperor should be described as a patron and recipient of religious counsel, not automatically as an ordinary disciple in the monastic sense.

梁武帝应被描述为宗教劝诫的接受者与政治 patron，而不能自动写成僧团意义上的普通弟子。

Interesting things

A monk with many faces · 多面僧人Sacred biography · 神异传记

Later stories say Baozhi could appear in different places at once and predict political events. These belong to sacred biography, not independently verifiable observation.

后世故事说宝志能同时出现在不同地方，并预言政治事件。这些属于神异传记，不能作为独立核验的观察记录。

Freedom to speak · 说真话的自由Historical reading · 历史解读

His unpredictable manner gave him room to speak to power in ways that ordinary officials could not. That social role is more historically useful than proving any miracle.

他难以预测的行为，使他能够用普通官员无法使用的方式面对权力。这种社会角色比证明神迹更有历史意义。

06 · 507–581 · Southern Chen–Sui · Three-Treatise teacher and lecturer

Falang 法朗

Xinghuang Temple · 兴皇寺

Associated temple landscape; no secure portrait · 相关寺院景观，未见可靠肖像

Temple connections

Xinghuang Temple · 兴皇寺Resided and taught there · 在此居住与讲学

Falang lectured at Xinghuang Temple for approximately twenty-five years. The temple became so closely associated with him that he was remembered as Xinghuang Falang.

法朗在兴皇寺讲学约二十五年。这个寺院与他的联系非常紧密，后世甚至以“兴皇法朗”称之。

Sheshan tradition · 摄山学系Inherited a teaching network · 继承思想网络

His authority also came from the Sheshan Three-Treatise tradition. This is a lineage and intellectual connection, not a claim that every teacher lived at Xinghuang.

他的权威也来自摄山三论学传统。这是师承与思想联系，并不意味着每一位老师都住在兴皇寺。

Deeds

Explained the three treatises · 讲解三论Madhyamaka learning · 中观学问

He taught the Middle Treatise, Twelve-Gate Treatise, and Hundred Treatise, showing students how to avoid turning ‘emptiness’ into another fixed doctrine.

他讲授《中论》《十二门论》《百论》，教导学生不要把“空”本身又变成一个固定的教条。

Made a school through teaching · 以讲学建立学派Long-term institutional work · 长期制度工作

His major action was educational: a sustained lecture career trained the generation that made Three-Treatise thought into a durable Chinese Buddhist tradition.

他的主要行动是教育性的：长期讲学培养出一代学者，使三论思想成为持久的中国佛教传统。

Titles and nicknames

Xinghuang Lang · 兴皇朗Temple-based name · 以寺为名

This name records his institutional home and teaching authority rather than a separate imperial title.

这个称呼记录了他的寺院归属与讲学权威，而不是另一个帝王授予的官号。

Fuhulang · 伏虎朗Later nickname · 后世外号

The ‘tiger-subduing’ nickname turns intellectual force into a vivid image. It should be read as a later memory device, not a literal biography.

“伏虎”外号把思想力量变成生动形象。它应被视为后世记忆方式，而非字面意义上的传记事实。

Masters and disciples

Sengquan · 僧诠Teacher in the Sheshan tradition · 摄山传统老师

Falang inherited the Sheshan Three-Treatise teaching associated with Sengquan.

法朗继承了与僧诠相关的摄山三论学传统。

Jizang · 吉藏Major disciple · 重要弟子

Jizang became the best-known student shaped by Falang’s lectures and later systematized the school’s philosophy.

吉藏是受法朗讲学影响最深、也最著名的弟子，后来系统化了这一学派的哲学。

Interesting things

What did he defeat? · 他究竟战胜了什么Student question · 给学生的问题

The memorable ‘tiger’ image can be turned into a question: perhaps the tiger was rigid definition, confusion, or the habit of treating concepts as things.

“伏虎”形象可以转化为一个问题：他战胜的也许不是老虎，而是僵化定义、混乱，或把概念当成实体的习惯。

A temple as a school · 寺院也是学校Historical effect · 历史影响

Xinghuang was not only a ritual site. Long lectures turned its rooms into a place where arguments, texts, and teacher–student relationships accumulated over time.

兴皇寺不只是礼仪场所。长期讲学使它成为观点、文本和师生关系不断积累的学校。

07 · 549–623 · Chen–Sui–Tang · Three-Treatise philosopher and systematizer

Jizang 吉藏

Xinghuang Temple · 兴皇寺

Later traditional representation · 后世传统画像

Temple connections

Xinghuang Temple · 兴皇寺Formative Nanjing temple · 建康重要学习寺院

Jizang followed Falang at Xinghuang Temple from childhood. This is his formative Nanjing study and teaching environment.

吉藏自幼随法朗在兴皇寺学习。这里是他在建康形成思想并参与讲学的重要环境。

Jiaxiang Temple · 嘉祥寺Later residence elsewhere · 后期外地寺院

His famous title Jiaxiang Master comes from Jiaxiang Temple near present-day Shaoxing, not from a Nanjing temple. The distinction matters.

他最著名的“嘉祥大师”称号来自今绍兴附近的嘉祥寺，而不是南京寺院。这个区别很重要。

Deeds

Systematized Sanlun · 系统化三论学Sanlun xuanyi · 《三论玄义》

He organized several generations of Three-Treatise teaching into a coherent philosophical system and wrote more than forty works.

他把数代三论学说整理成较为完整的哲学体系，并著述四十余部。

Explained the two truths · 解释二谛Language and ultimate reality · 语言与究竟实相

He argued that conventional language is useful but must not be mistaken for ultimate reality. The teaching resists attachment to both ordinary concepts and supposedly final doctrines.

他认为世俗语言有用，但不能被误认为究竟实相。这一思想同时反对执着于普通概念和所谓最终教条。

Titles and nicknames

Jiaxiang Master · 嘉祥大师Temple-based honorific · 以寺院为名的尊称

This title refers to a later temple connection near Shaoxing. It should not be used as shorthand for his entire Nanjing career.

这个称号指向绍兴附近的后期寺院联系，不应被用来概括他全部的建康经历。

Hu Jizang · 胡吉藏Family background · 家族背景

The name Hu reflects the Central or Western Asian ancestry remembered in his family history; it is not a separate monastic title.

“胡”字反映了传记所记的中亚或西域家族背景，并不是另一个僧号。

Masters and disciples

Falang · 法朗Principal master · 主要师父

Falang was the teacher who shaped Jizang’s early formation at Xinghuang Temple.

法朗是在兴皇寺塑造吉藏早期学术训练的主要老师。

Korean and Chinese disciples · 中韩弟子网络Transmission beyond Jiankang · 传播超出建康

Huiguan and other students carried Three-Treatise learning into Korea and Japan, turning a Nanjing intellectual formation into an international lineage.

慧灌等弟子把三论学传入朝鲜半岛和日本，使在建康形成的思想成为跨区域传承。

Interesting things

A child in the lecture hall · 讲堂里的孩子Early education · 早期教育

A traditional account says his father brought him to Falang’s lectures and that he entered the monastic community at about seven. The story makes the temple feel like a school as well as a monastery.

传统记载说，他的父亲带他去听法朗讲经，他约七岁进入僧团。这个故事让寺院既像寺院，也像学校。

Not attachment to the right answer · 不执着于“正确答案”Philosophical habit · 哲学训练

Jizang’s arguments repeatedly unsettle fixed categories. His greatness lies in using doctrine to loosen attachment to doctrine itself.

吉藏的论述不断松动固定分类。他的伟大在于用教义来解除对教义本身的执着。

08 · 594–657 · Sui–Tang · Mountain scholar, meditation teacher, and community organizer

Farong 法融

Youqi Temple · 幽栖寺

Associated mountain landscape; no secure portrait · 相关山林景观，未见可靠肖像

Temple connections

Foku Temple · 佛窟寺Studied its collection · 在此研读藏书

Farong studied at Foku Temple on Niutou Mountain, traditionally said to have an extensive collection. The connection is one of study, not necessarily his principal residence.

法融在牛头山佛窟寺研读，传统上说寺中藏书丰富。这里应标注为学习联系，不一定是他的主要居所。

Youqi Temple · 幽栖寺Principal retreat and teaching centre · 主要隐修与讲学中心

He established a community at Youqi Temple and attracted more than one hundred students. This is the clearest institutional home of his mature teaching.

他在幽栖寺建立共同体，吸引一百多名学生。这是他成熟时期最明确的制度性中心。

Jianchu Temple · 建初寺Later teaching and death · 后期讲学与圆寂之处

Later tradition places lectures and his death at Jianchu Temple. It should be presented as a late connection, not the same as Youqi’s mountain community.

后世记载把他后期讲学与圆寂之事放在建初寺。这里应作为晚年联系，而不能与幽栖山林共同体混为一谈。

Deeds

Read across traditions · 广泛读书Scholar-hermit · 学者型隐修者

He studied Buddhist scriptures, Chinese classics, history, Daoist writings, and medical works. His learning was broad before it became a Chan lineage.

他研读佛经、中国经典、历史、道家著作与医学书籍。他先是广博的学者，后来才成为禅学传承人物。

Fed a mountain community · 供养山中共同体Daily institutional labor · 日常制度劳动

During unrest and food shortage, he reportedly begged for grain in the city and carried it back for hundreds of people. The story makes administration and care part of religious greatness.

动荡与粮食短缺时期，据传他每天进城乞粮，再背回去供养数百人。这个故事把管理与照料也纳入宗教伟大。

Titles and nicknames

Niutou Farong · 牛头法融Mountain-based name · 以山为名

这个称呼把他与牛头山及早期牛头禅传统联系起来。

‘Lazy Rong’ appears in later Chan stories that turn his refusal to perform empty social gestures into a teaching image.

“懒融”见于后世禅宗故事，把他不愿做空洞礼节的行为转化为一种教化形象。

史料对他早期老师的名字有不同写法。这里应保留记载差异，而不要假装只有一种确定答案。

传统上把法融视为道信弟子，并以智岩为重要继承者。现代研究对正式会面与传法保持谨慎。

后世故事说，法融遇道信前鸟雀衔花而来，悟道后鸟雀不再出现。这是关于神迹与符号的传说，不是可核验事件。

他最有用的历史形象也许是背粮食供养他人：宗教领袖的价值，也可以用共同体能否生存来衡量。

Fayan Wenyi 法眼文益

Qingliang Temple · 清凉寺

Temple connections

Bao’en Chan Temple · 报恩禅院Early Southern Tang connection · 南唐早期寺院联系

Fayan taught in the Southern Tang capital and was associated with Bao’en Chan Temple before the Qingliang phase of his career.

法眼在南唐都城讲学，并与报恩禅院有联系，之后才进入清凉寺时期。

Qingliang Temple · 清凉寺Principal Nanjing teaching centre · 建康主要讲学中心

Qingliang Temple became the principal site of his mature teaching and the ancestral place remembered by the Fayan school.

清凉寺成为他成熟时期主要的讲学中心，也成为后世法眼宗所记的祖庭。

Deeds

Wrote ten warnings for the Chan school · 撰写十规论Zongmen shigui lun · 《宗门十规论》

He criticized teachers who imitated old gestures, misunderstood scripture, or claimed enlightenment without training. His rules made institutional quality a religious issue.

他批评模仿古德动作、误解经典或未经训练便自称开悟的教师。他把制度质量变成宗教问题。

Built a lineage · 建立传承网络One of the Five Houses · 禅宗五家之一

His teaching network became the Fayan school, one of the Five Houses of Chan, with successors across the Southern Tang and beyond.

他的讲学网络发展为法眼宗，成为禅宗五家之一，弟子与传承遍及南唐及更广区域。

Titles and nicknames

Qingliang Wenyi · 清凉文益Temple-based name · 以寺为名

这个称呼保留了他在建康最重要的讲学基地。

Fayan Chan Master · 法眼禅师Posthumous honorific · 谥号式尊称

“法眼”成为身后尊称，后来又成为宗派名称。它不是他年轻时使用的称号。

Luohan Guichen · 罗汉桂琛Principal master · 主要老师

桂琛被记为法眼的主要老师，也是他后来发展传承的来源。

天台德韶等后继者把他的教法带入南唐与吴越世界；后世记录统计了许多法嗣。

Interesting things

Fayan’s warning is surprisingly modern: repeating an impressive phrase or gesture is not the same as understanding the practice behind it.

法眼的警告很现代：重复一句漂亮话或一个神秘动作，并不等于理解其背后的修行。

他的十规说明，寺院若要保持传承可信度，就需要为老师、文本和开悟宣称建立标准。

10 · d. 1593 · Late Ming · Huayan teacher and reviver of Qixia

Su’an Zhenjie 素庵真节

Qixia Temple · 栖霞寺

Associated Qixia site photograph; no secure portrait · 栖霞寺相关现代摄影，未见可靠肖像

Temple connections

Qixia Temple · 栖霞寺Resided and rebuilt there · 在此居住与重建

Zhenjie resided at Qixia from approximately the 1550s until his death. This is the clearest temple residence in the first ten profiles.

真节约从十六世纪五十年代起居住在栖霞寺，直到去世。这是前十位人物中最明确的寺院居住关系。

Yuantong Jingshe · 圆通精舍Educational and devotional space · 教育与修行空间

He created the Yuantong Jingshe within the wider Qixia revival, showing that institutional renewal could involve new rooms and programs as well as repairs.

他在栖霞复兴过程中创建圆通精舍，说明制度复兴不仅是修房屋，也包括新的空间与教育项目。

Deeds

Taught across schools · 融通诸宗讲学Huayan, Tiantai, Yogācāra, Chan, Pure Land · 华严、天台、唯识、禅、净土

He lectured on Huayan while also teaching Tiantai, Yogācāra, Chan, and Pure Land materials. His work made Qixia a broad teaching centre rather than a narrow sectarian base.

他以华严为主，也讲授天台、唯识、禅与净土内容。他使栖霞成为开放的综合性讲学中心，而不是狭窄的宗派基地。

Made revival financially possible · 让复兴能够持续Fields, gardens, and donors · 田地、园林与施主

He repaired halls, cultivated land and gardens, secured changsheng fields, and built relationships with officials, scholars, merchants, and donors. These were the material conditions of teaching.

他修缮殿堂、经营田园、取得长生田，并与官员、学者、商人和施主建立联系。这些是讲学能够持续的物质条件。

Titles and nicknames

Su’an Zhenjie · 素庵真节Dharma name and style · 法名与号

Su’an is his style or religious name; Zhenjie is the Dharma name. They should not be mistaken for a court title.

素庵是其号或宗教称呼，真节是法名。两者都不应被误写成朝廷官号。

Reviver of Qixia · 栖霞中兴人物Historical description · 历史描述

‘Reviver’ describes his historical effect on the monastery. It is a modern analytical label rather than a formal title from his lifetime.

“中兴人物”描述的是他对寺院的历史影响，是现代分析性称谓，而不是生前正式称号。

Masters and disciples

Huayan and Linji networks · 华严与临济网络Combined traditions · 复合传统

Later sources connect Zhenjie with Huayan learning and a Linji Chan environment. His career is better shown as a network than reduced to one exclusive school.

后世资料把真节与华严学及临济禅环境联系起来。他的经历更适合展示为网络，而不是归入唯一宗派。

Guxin Ruxin is connected with Zhenjie’s teaching environment and later became a major Vinaya reformer. The relationship should be presented as a teaching connection, not inflated into a complete genealogy without further proof.

古心如馨与真节的讲学环境有关，后来成为重要的律学复兴者。这里应标注为师承联系，不应在缺少更多证据时扩展成完整谱系。

Interesting things

真节的故事让复兴的实际一面变得可见：名寺要重新成为学校，必须有田产、修缮、学生和施主。
