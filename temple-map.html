Nanjing Buddhist Landscapes

A city of temples, sites, and memory.

Click a point to place a temple in Nanjing’s present geography. The map uses OpenStreetMap coordinates for current mapped sites; historical foundations and earlier footprints require separate archaeological verification.

How to read this map
Red points mark present mapped sites. The image and notes describe the site; they do not claim that every surviving building belongs to the same historical period.
